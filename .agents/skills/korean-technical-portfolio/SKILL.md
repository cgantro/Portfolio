# Korean Technical Portfolio
Per project: explain purpose and context; map implemented architecture and boundaries; characterize the technical problem; show observed evidence and cause analysis; describe genuine options and rationale; document specific implementation, measurement and limitations. Distinguish individual work from team output. Source every major claim with a file/commit/measurement, and preserve test setup, units and uncertain outcomes. Do not fabricate graphs, diagrams, screenshot data or benchmarks.

Design for two reading speeds: a concise overview that lets a reviewer recognize the capability, and supporting technical detail for a developer interview. Position real diagrams and measured charts alongside the explanation they support. Use precise professional Korean with coherent compound sentences; do not overcompress into terse bullets.

Before publication compare prose, charts, captions and source code. Optionally run hiring-sim-portfolio-review and redteam; humanize-korean can be used conservatively after technical facts are fixed. Layout and visuals are handled separately by design skills.

## Case-study structure
For a project write-up, use the sections that are supported by evidence: context and problem, constraints, investigation and alternatives, the author's contribution, implementation, verification, measured outcome, and remaining limitations. Keep it concise when the project does not need every section. Link claims to source files, tests, benchmark records, or user-confirmed experience. Do not invent business context, user research, screenshots, team members, outcomes, or metrics. Separate measured results from hypotheses and planned work.
